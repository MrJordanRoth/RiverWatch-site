Privacy Policy for RiverWatch

Last updated: August 24, 2026

Data Collection & Storage

RiverWatch does not collect, track, transmit, or sell any personal user data. All configuration preferences (such as monitored station codes, custom threshold values, alert preferences, and UI theme selections) are saved locally in your browser using chrome.storage.

Third-Party Services

To retrieve live hydrologic data and manage optional Pro tier accounts, RiverWatch communicates with the following external endpoints:

  • NOAA Water Prediction Service (api.water.noaa.gov): Fetches observed river stage levels, flow rates, forecast points, and station metadata.
  • National Weather Service (api.weather.gov): Fetches active flood watches, warnings, and weather advisories for gauge coordinates.
  • ExtensionPay & Stripe (extensionpay.com): Handles secure account verification and payment processing for RiverWatch Pro. Payment card data is processed directly by Stripe and is never seen or stored by RiverWatch.

Contact
