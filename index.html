RiverWatch

Real-time NOAA river stage tracking, interactive hydrograph trend charts, flood threshold monitoring, and desktop hazard alerts in your browser.

Interactive Hydrographs

Smooth, High-DPI canvas charts with hover tooltips, dashed forecast curves, and 3D / 7D / 14D timeframe selector controls.

Dynamic Flood Stages

Auto-extracts official NWS Action, Minor, Moderate, and Major flood thresholds directly onto the popup and chart backdrop.

Desktop Flood Alerts

Instant browser notifications when monitored stations cross flood thresholds or receive active NWS watches and warnings.

Store custom station labels and seamlessly switch between monitored river gauges directly from the extension header.
