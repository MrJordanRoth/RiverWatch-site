About RiverWatch

RiverWatch is a lightweight Manifest V3 Chrome extension designed for kayakers, anglers, river enthusiasts, and flood-prone communities. It pulls data directly from official NOAA water prediction endpoints to deliver instant stage height readings without requiring complex web navigation.

Key Features

  • Live Toolbar Badge: Real-time river stage height color-coded by official NOAA flood severity categories.
  • Interactive Hydrograph: Smooth 3-day historical trend graph with precise hover tooltips.
  • Flow & Threshold Metrics: Discharge rates (kcfs) and official flood stages (Action, Minor, Moderate, Major).
  • Custom Station Selection: Monitor any 5-character NWSLI gauge in the United States.
